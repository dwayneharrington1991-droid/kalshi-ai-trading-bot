## Recovers automatically
- **8768 process dies:** relaunched from the last healthy snapshot (same build, journal and environment) after two
  down checks, never during a guarded deploy, at most 3 times in 6 h. After that it stops and alerts you.
- **Alert service crashes:** systemd restarts it within 10 s. It never resends a market already sent.
- **Oracle reboots:** the alert service, watchdog and forward-test timers start at boot, and the watchdog relaunches 8768.
- **Telegram you will receive from the watchdog:** stale data, no opening call for 17 min, alert service down,
  failed delivery, no settlements for 2 h, training errors, disk under 5 GB, CPU above 97% for about 16 min.
  Each is sent once, plus a "RECOVERED" message.

## Known risks
- **CPU headroom:** one core at about 45–75%. An open dashboard tab costs more, which is reduced by `c0072a5`.
  Deploys must pass the 85% gate.
- **Disk:** 19 GB free; the journal is 2.4 GB. The watchdog alerts below 5 GB.
- **Watchdog relaunch:** the path was tested on a dummy server only. A real relaunch has not happened, by design.
- **Scope:** the v5 retrain and forward tests run unattended, but interpreting results and any promotion need a
  session and your approval.
